Privacy Policy

Course Kit Pty Ltd provides hosted services ("Cloud Apps") for Atlassian Cloud Products. The Apps are delivered through the Atlassian Connect Forge framework ("Atlassian Forge"). Cloud Apps can be identified by the "Cloud" category in the corresponding Atlassian Marketplace listing.

Protection of Privacy

Protecting your privacy is of the utmost importance to Course Kit Pty Ltd. We are fully committed to maintaining the confidentiality of your data and personally identifiable information ("Customer Data").

To ensure compliance with data protection regulations, we have implemented rigorous technical and organizational measures. However, it is important to note that the transmission of data over the internet may carry inherent security vulnerabilities, and absolute protection against unauthorized access by third parties cannot be guaranteed.

Data Security

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, our Cloud Apps do not store Customer Data locally. Instead, they securely store Customer Data within the corresponding Atlassian Cloud Product.

For detailed information about the security practices of the Atlassian Cloud Product, please refer to the Atlassian Cloud Product Security Statement.

Access to Customer Data

Only authorized employees and subcontractors of Course Kit Pty Ltd's support and development teams have access to Customer Data. These subcontractors are contractually bound to adhere to the same strict data security and privacy standards that we uphold.

Subcontractors

We engage the following subcontractors:

  • Digital Ocean, LLC: Our Cloud Apps are hosted on Digital Ocean. Please review the Digital Ocean privacy statement for more information.
  • OpenAI OpCo, LLC: We utilize Open AI's GPT-3 for text generation. For details regarding data privacy, please consult the OpenAI privacy statement.
  • Better Stack, Inc.: We employ Better Stack LogTail product for error logging. Kindly refer to the Better Stack privacy statement to understand their privacy practices.
  • Amazon Web Services, Inc: Email notifications are sent via AWS Simple Email Service. Please refer to the AWS Privacy Notice for more information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to periodically modify this privacy policy in order to ensure compliance with current legal requirements or to implement changes to our services. Any changes to this policy will become effective as of the date they are posted on this page.

We strongly recommend that you periodically review this Privacy Policy for any updates. Changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page.

More Information

For more detailed information, please refer to the Atlassian Privacy Policy.
